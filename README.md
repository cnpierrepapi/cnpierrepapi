# Chukwudumaga Nnawuogo

I'm a design engineer. I build the part of an AI product that people actually touch, and I keep at it until someone who has never seen it can use it without asking me anything.

My training is in design thinking and human-computer interaction, not computer science. I write code with AI and always have. So the model is the engine, and my job is the dashboard: what goes on the screen, what stays off it, what the first thirty seconds feel like, and how plain the words can get before they stop being true.

If the job is a backend with no screen, or deep infrastructure work, I'm not your guy. If it's an AI product that real people have to understand, I am.

Founder of [Onenept Studios](https://onenept.com). Open to remote and contract work as a Design Engineer, UX Engineer or AI Product Engineer.

## Live now

**[AD FONTES](https://adfontes.fun)** · three.js, NEAR, Claude · on NEAR testnet

A world of small nations run by AI. Their citizens read real research papers, trade what they learn, and sometimes go to war. You fund one and become its god.

Under the hood it's all blockchain: DAOs, agent wallets, tokens, a contract that seals every turn. On screen there's none of that. You sign up with an email, a funded account gets made for you, and there's no wallet popup and no crypto words anywhere. You give "grace", not NEAR. The world is a 3D map you click around in. I wanted it to feel like any web game you'd send a friend, and only the README knows it's on chain. Built for the NEAR hackathon, submitted 12 Nov. Mainnet comes next.

**[Her Crown Garden](https://hercrowngarden.shop)** · client work · Next.js

A hair care shop for a Nigerian brand. Her customers find her on TikTok, on a phone, so I designed the phone version first and the desktop one after. People with 4C hair shop by porosity before anything else, so the whole catalogue splits on that, and orders go straight to WhatsApp because that's how her buyers already buy. I mocked up three directions and she picked one.

**[Warmleads](https://warmleads.app)** · Next.js, Supabase, Stripe, Paystack

Scored leads for freelancers. You search for the kind of business you want to work with, and each one comes with a score and its biggest need. To see a contact you press and hold a button, like confirming a payment in a crypto wallet. It replaced a credits system. Getting a redesign in 2027.

**[FUGITIVA](https://fugitiva.onenept.com)** · SvelteKit, three.js

An online journal of case files on women behind the crimes people assume men commit. Bauhaus layout, masks generated in code, real police photos.

**[OUTBRK](https://outbrk.fun)** · Vite, three.js

A coming-soon page with a virus in it, and a puzzle hidden in the virus.

## Built, not deployed

**[Melax](https://github.com/cnpierrepapi/melax)** · TypeScript, Lightning, Cashu

A bitcoin wallet for the small phone you already have. Dial a short code and a menu opens, in Wolof, French or English. No app, no internet. It was for the Dakar Bitcoin Hack and I chose not to submit it, so it runs on a local test network. The demo page has two drawn feature phones calling the real server, and its setup guide only ticks a step when the server's own SMS proves that step happened. Clicking ahead doesn't count.

## How I work

- Design for the first thirty seconds. If someone needs a tutorial, the screen is wrong.
- Hide the plumbing. Nobody should have to learn what a wallet, a token or an API is to use the thing.
- Build for one named person first. Then check it on a phone, on the live site, not on my laptop.
- Say what's real and what isn't, in the README, before anyone has to ask.
